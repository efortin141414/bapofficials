# V6.44 — Digital ID Name Bold + Back Details Alignment

This update improves the visual formatting of the Digital ID.

## What changed

- The **member name on the Digital ID front** is now more visibly **bold** and emphasized.
- The **back information fields** were repositioned and aligned better with the printed detail areas.
- The **email field on the back** keeps readable casing instead of forcing all-uppercase.
- The alignment improvements were applied to both the **default back layout** and **custom uploaded back layout**.

## Result

The Digital ID front now gives stronger emphasis to the member name, while the back-side emergency information lines up more cleanly with the intended detail fields.

## SQL needed?

No additional SQL is required for this update.
